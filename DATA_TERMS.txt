TERMS FOR THE SCAN DATA IN THIS DATASET
=======================================

Short version: the author of these scans has stated publicly that they may be
downloaded and used without copyright restrictions. This mirror passes that on
unchanged. It adds no restrictions of its own and grants no rights beyond what
the author has stated. Please credit the scan project and the holding museum.

1. WHAT THIS DATASET IS
-----------------------

An unofficial mirror of threedscans.com, an archive of 3D scans of museum
objects initiated in 2012 by the artist Oliver Laric, produced in collaboration
with museums across Europe.

The files are mirrored exactly as served by threedscans.com. Nothing has been
re-meshed, re-encoded, or converted. The mirror claims no copyright, database
right, or other right in the scans or in this collection of them.

2. WHAT THE AUTHOR HAS SAID
---------------------------

threedscans.com itself carries no licensing or terms-of-use text. As of July
2026 there is no such statement on its homepage, its Info page, or any item
page.

The permission relied on here is Oliver Laric's own statement on his companion
project Lincoln 3D Scans (https://www.lincoln3dscans.co.uk/info), run with The
Collection in Lincoln, to the effect that all models there can be downloaded and
used without copyright restrictions. Published accounts of threedscans.com
report the same intent for the wider archive: that Laric asserts no copyright
over the scans and intends them to circulate freely.

3. THE LIMITS OF THAT, STATED PLAINLY
-------------------------------------

a. The explicit statement is published for the Lincoln project. It is strong
   evidence of intent for the whole archive, but threedscans.com carries no
   equivalent text of its own.

b. It is a statement that no restrictions are asserted. It is not a formal
   license instrument. This dataset therefore is NOT tagged CC0 or PDDL: those
   are dedications that only the rights holder can execute, and the author has
   not executed one in those terms.

c. The underlying objects are overwhelmingly public-domain works. However, some
   jurisdictions may recognise rights in a 3D scan of a public-domain work, and
   holding institutions may impose their own conditions, particularly on
   commercial reuse. If your use is commercial, check with the relevant
   institution.

d. Nothing here is legal advice. If your use depends on the answer, seek your
   own.

4. ATTRIBUTION
--------------

Credit both the scan project and the institution holding the object. Example:

    Scan by Oliver Laric, threedscans.com. Object: Siren, 4th century CE,
    marble, National Archaeological Museum Athens.

The file metadata.json in this repository gives the title, period, material,
holding institution, scan year, and scan method for every object, so per-file
attribution can be generated automatically.

5. TAKEDOWN
-----------

If you are Oliver Laric, or an institution holding one of these objects, and you
want material removed or these terms restated, open a discussion on this dataset
and it will be taken down promptly and without argument.
